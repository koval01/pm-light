# Code of conduct

This project is a place to work on a mail client together. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests, reviews
and discussions, is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, with newcomers above all;
- argue about code and ideas, never about people;
- accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and say why;
- keep other people's private information private, including anything seen in a bug report or a log.

Not accepted: harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexual language or imagery, threats, and
publishing anyone's private details without their consent.

Reports go to the maintainer, Yaroslav Koval ([@koval01](https://github.com/koval01)), through a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/koval01/pm-light/security/advisories/new) marked as a conduct matter, and are
handled in confidence. A maintainer may edit or remove anything that breaks these rules, and may block anyone who
keeps breaking them.
